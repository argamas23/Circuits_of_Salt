# Master Bibliography for Circuits of Salt

Prepared 26 October 2026. Author–date style; source titles retain their published spelling.

This register contains **117 entries** assembled from the repository’s source files, the project’s paper and proposal reference lists, source leads in Bibliography.pdf, and earlier conversation material preserved in THESIS_CONTEXT.md. It also includes works needed to identify the theorists discussed in Chapter 1. Bibliographic presence, direct consultation and support for a particular claim are different things; the notes distinguish them.

Every top-level PDF in Books/, References/ and Bibliography/ is accounted for. The source inventory also records the corpus-only salt-industry text and separately lists generated figures. References within every monograph are not recursively treated as the researcher’s reading. The related thesis’s 160 references and the local course syllabus’s reading list are supplied separately as unverified secondary leads.

This is a master research bibliography, not a declaration that every item has been read or belongs in Chapter 1. The chapter has a shorter reference list containing only its 14 cited works. No source count here should replace the computational corpus count.

Verification descriptions refer to what was checked: title pages, a catalogue, a publisher record, or a relevant passage. A verified bibliographic record does not establish that every historical assertion is supported. Entries marked **verification required** remain incomplete. No original archival consultation, publication acceptance or repository deposit is inferred from a working manuscript.

Coverage of earlier chats is limited to this conversation and the saved project records available here. Unseen chat history cannot be searched. For online records consulted in this pass, access date: 26 October 2026.
