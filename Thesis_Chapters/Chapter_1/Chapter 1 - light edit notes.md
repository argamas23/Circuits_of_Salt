## Content retained

The original network-backbone assertion and the reference to Foreign Department records are restored. Repeated summaries remain throughout, including the conclusion.

Ambiguous wording such as “floral extraction”, the inconsistent definitions of netsang, and claims previously flagged for source checking are preserved for a later content review. No new historical or theoretical claims, references, or geographical identifications were introduced.
