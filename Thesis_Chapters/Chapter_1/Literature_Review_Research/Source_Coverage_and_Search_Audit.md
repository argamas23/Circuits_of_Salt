**Access language:** ‘full text’ means relevant original text was accessible and read to the extent specified in a memo; ‘indexed’ means text returned from an index of the original rather than a successfully retrieved complete copy; ‘metadata/abstract’ does not support a detailed account of a work’s argument; ‘lead’ is not a verified original. Printed and PDF page numbers are distinguished in the evidence memos.

## Additional web sources already recorded in the project

The politics memo records direct links and access results for Kanum, Nachar, Fairs and Festivals/Lavi, Mamgain’s Kinnaur gazetteer, the 1954 agreement, Beszterda, and the supplied 2026 news. It separates a catalogue check from verification of a requested passage.

- **Kanum/Nachar/fairs:** exact passage and monograph-designation uncertainties remain where the original scans were not obtained. Nachar catalogue numbering differs between records; do not silently select an edition. Publication dates do not automatically date every observation.
- **1954 agreement:** the official UN text is the appropriate authority for the agreement’s date, specified passes, and term. The term alone does not establish when each corridor ceased operating. Do not reuse Beszterda’s erroneous footnote date.
- **Contemporary Shipki-la reports:** Business Today and Amar Ujala report different permitted commodity counts. Official notifications are needed before choosing a number or presenting tax entitlements as established. Planned November 2026 Lavi events are future relative to this review date.
- **Documentary and Nepali Times:** usable for contemporary representation and source leads, with provenance and chronological qualifications. Neither substitutes for the relevant ethnography.

## Search approach and representative queries

Searches began with the existing cited chapter, continuity note, previous review, 40-item bibliography, master bibliography, local Books/References files, and corpus OCR. Existing project manuscripts and computational outputs were used as context and leads, not independent corroboration.

The search combined exact-title retrieval, thematic searching, and backward reference-tracing. Queries included:

Results were followed into publishers, institutional repositories, original journal sites, official census/treaty sources, or author-shared originals where available. Search-result pages are not used as final citations. News, vendor descriptions, popular pink-salt material, and geology unrelated to the historical questions were not treated as interchangeable academic evidence.

This log records search families and principal outcomes rather than every engine ranking or failed URL. It is sufficient to disclose the review’s scope; it is not a reproducible systematic-review protocol or proof that unlocated work does not exist.
